Control Stack Notes

A quick reminder of what each paper-trading control changes.

Starting Cash

Total simulated account balance before any paper trades run.

Max Trade Size

Caps how much available cash a single buy can deploy.

Max Daily Loss

Stops new buys after realized losses reach the limit.

Max Trades Per Day

Limits how many paper entries the optimizer can open.

Gain Reserve %

Saves a percentage of profitable realized exits as a tax-safety planning bucket.

Lookback Window

Number of recent prices used to judge lows, highs, dips, and momentum.

Buy Dip %

Minimum drop from a recent high before a rebound can become a buy setup.

Rebound Confirm %

Minimum recovery from a recent low before the optimizer buys.

Profit Target %

Gain from entry that triggers a paper sell to lock in profit.

Stop Loss %

Loss from entry that triggers a protective paper sell.

Trailing Stop %

Allowed pullback from the highest price seen after entry.

Kill Switch

Rejects every trade, useful for testing the safety layer.
