Reference Card

Control Stack Notes

A quick reminder of what each trading control changes.

Starting Cash

Total local account mirror used for sizing and exposure calculations.

Max Trade Size

Caps how much available cash a single buy can deploy.

Max Daily Loss %

Stops new buys after realized losses reach this percentage of starting cash.

Cooldown Sec

Wait time after a losing exit before another buy can be approved.

Target Profit %

Exits when price reaches this percentage above the actual buy price.

Entry Score %

Momentum Confidence Score threshold used to open a trade. It no longer forces exits.

Entry Interval Sec

After a buy remains open for this long, another qualifying momentum entry may open a separate lot.

Max Open Trades

Caps the number of independently targeted lots that may be open at the same time.

Gain Reserve %

Saves a percentage of profitable realized exits as a tax-safety planning bucket.

Catastrophic Loss %

Emergency loss threshold that can force a protective sell.

Kill Switch

Rejects every trade, useful for testing the safety layer.
